How accurate is it?

We trained many algorithms for every model and kept the best. Scores come from 5-fold cross-validation, which tests on data the model has not seen.

Data cleaning

  • Removed 4,616 duplicate symptom rows (4,920 to 304 unique).
  • Fixed column names and disease spellings.
  • Replaced impossible zeros (blood pressure, BMI, glucose) with the median.
  • Removed 723 duplicate rows and fixed an inverted label in the heart data.

Three models

  • Symptoms to one of 41 diseases (Random Forest).
  • Heart risk from two datasets, averaged.
  • Diabetes risk from glucose, BP, BMI, age.

Risk level

For heart and diabetes the model gives a probability.

under 30% Low · 30–60% Intermediate · 60%+ High

For each disease from symptoms we combine the model’s probability with how many of the disease’s typical symptoms you have.

score = 0.6 × probability + 0.4 × symptom match

Certainty

Certainty says how sure the model is. If many diseases share your symptoms, the probability is shared and certainty is lower. Add more symptoms to raise it.

Values you skip are estimated and can lower certainty by one level.

Why this result?

For each value we swap your number for a healthy one and see how much the risk falls. The biggest drops are the factors that pushed your risk up.
